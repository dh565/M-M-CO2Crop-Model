1                          ! rec69 canopy_sink_lai: the last of the six canopy steps and the only one that moves state. 0 = off (default). 1 = on; requires rec64, rec65, rec66, rec67 and rec68, and with all six on the canopy stops being a bookkeeping product of carbon and becomes a sink. WHAT CHANGES: (1) LAI stops being lai = W_green * sla_from_dvs(dvs2) and becomes the sink green lamina area index that the carbon-blind organ law of rec64-rec68 builds; (2) lamina carbon stops being a partition fraction and becomes an area demand dA * LMA, where dA is the increment of the monotone cumulative built lamina area, so senesced area is never re-charged and never refunded; (3) the remainder of dm_gain goes to stem, root and storage on the retained anchored fractions renormalised over those three, which still sum to exactly 1, so the machine-zero carbon closure is untouched; (4) unmet demand is carried as a hard non-negative backlog and the demographic limiter acts on future demand only. THE LIMITER IS DEMOGRAPHIC, NOT A SCALING, and that is measured: over a 2.6-fold PPFD range (111/191/286 umol m-2 s-1) leaf elongation rate was not affected by PPFD and maximum leaf width was not influenced by temperature or PPFD (Bos 1999 thesis, doi 10.18174/198200, Ch. 2), so the two quantities a carbon scaling would have to move are measured not to move. What does respond is demographic: 75 % shade cuts main-stem final leaf number from 9 to 8, and to 7 in 38 % of plants at 508 plants m-2 (P < 0.001) (Evers 2006 thesis, doi 10.18174/24302, Ch. 3 Fig. 4 p. 57), and the same 75 % shade drives the probability of primary-tiller occurrence to 0-0.3 (Ch. 4 Table 2 p. 85). So the limiter (i) suspends tiller initiation, (ii) aborts cohorts that have not yet produced a ligulated leaf - parameter-free, and it destroys no area ever built, whereas a rate-based abortion of established cohorts would need an invented constant and is deliberately not implemented - and (iii) cuts the emergent final leaf number by at most 2 leaves, the measured maximum in that source, counting one leaf per new tip appearing while the backlog is open, so the leaf removed is the last one and is not yet tipped. No tipped leaf is ever scaled. The Haun clock keeps running, so the senescence chronology Sl = HS - Gl is untouched. INVARIANTS: carbon and nitrogen balances still close at machine zero, the water balance stays within 1 mm, Richards failures are 0, the non-leaf fractions renormalise to exactly 1, the backlog is hard non-negative and reported, and no tipped leaf is scaled. The properly matched measurement is used instead: Sales et al. 2022 (J Exp Bot 73(10):3221-3237, doi 10.1093/jxb/erac096, Table 1), field-grown wheat, two lines, two years, flag-leaf lamina, LMA in g m-2 - booting 52.7/53.0, anthesis 47.0/50.0, grain filling 50.7/47.6. csl_LMA = 48.5 g m-2 is the anthesis mean of the two lines; bracket 47.0-53.0.
